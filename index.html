About me

I'm a Computer Science student at Howard University who lives where design meets engineering — turning ideas into products people genuinely enjoy using.

I came to product design by building things of my own. Founding REVEL my freshman year taught me how an idea becomes a product: knowing who it's for, shaping the brand, designing every piece, and getting it to launch.

Internships at Nybble Group and VDart brought that mindset into UX — designing accessible features for children with disabilities and interfaces for an AI-powered résumé platform, alongside designers, developers and product teams.

As a Figma Campus Leader, I launched the first HBCU chapter at Howard and run workshops that help other students grow as designers. I'm looking for opportunities in product design, UI/UX, front-end development and creative technology — especially teams building AI-powered products.
